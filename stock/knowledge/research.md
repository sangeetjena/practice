# Master-agent research reference

This file contains general guidance, not current market facts or proven trading rules.

- A volume expansion confirms activity; it does not establish the cause or guarantee continuation.
- A candle pattern needs completed bars and context. A single candle is weak evidence.
- Bollinger bands measure relative price position and volatility. Touching a band alone does not prove reversal.
- A company valuation snapshot can be old. State its fetched_at timestamp.
- News summaries must separate reports from speculation and cite their source articles.
- Historical critique can suggest a hypothesis; improvements require independent evaluation.
- Abstain when evidence is insufficient. Confidence is subjective, not calibrated probability.
